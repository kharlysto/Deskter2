GOOD QUESTIONS

Before you get started.

Can I use Deskter without paying?

Yes. Account creation, discovery, listings, saves, follows, and inquiries are part of the free core. Pro and Boost are optional future purchases. Marketplace transaction fees have not been decided; checkout is not enabled.

Can customers pay for services here yet?

Not yet. This build supports service discovery and inquiries. Payment collection, refunds, and seller payouts require an approved provider and additional implementation.

Does Pro include Boost?

No. Pro is planned as a subscription for seller tools. Boost is planned as a separate purchase for sponsored visibility. Neither guarantees sales or verification.

Is this a launched production marketplace?

No. This is a development build. Public launch still requires business and payment onboarding, operational safeguards, and deployment verification.
